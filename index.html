Flagship Project
Independent Portfolio Project · Graduation Research

GIS-Based Industrial Development Readiness Model (IDRM)

Spatial intelligence for understanding industrial growth and future readiness across Dammam's three industrial cities.

Role Team Leader & GIS Lead
Project Type Independent Portfolio Project · Research-Based
Tools ArcGIS Pro · ArcGIS Online · Power BI · Spatial Analysis

The question

Dammam's three industrial cities have grown for over two decades, but does current industrial strength necessarily indicate readiness for future development? This research set out to measure readiness itself, not just present-day scale.

How the model was built

Using 25 years of spatial and temporal data across 120 factory records and nine industry classifications, the model combines industrial structure, accessibility, and growth into a composite score — built with Kernel Density Estimation, Location Quotient, weighted overlay, buffer analysis, and sensitivity analysis.

What the spatial analysis revealed

The three cities form a complementary — not competing — industrial ecosystem, each with its own specialization. But once industrial structure, accessibility, and growth are weighted together into a single readiness score, the ranking of the cities shifts.

What this means for industrial readiness

D3 records the strongest composite readiness under the model's indicators, even though D1 remains the more established industrial base today — a distinction that matters when planning where future infrastructure investment will have the most impact.
