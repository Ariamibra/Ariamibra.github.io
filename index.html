01 — ABOUT

Spatial thinking,
decision-ready outputs.

I’m a GIS graduate focused on spatial analysis, geospatial data, and decision support. My strongest work sits where structured data, spatial relationships, and practical questions meet.

My capstone began as a team study of Dammam’s industrial cities from 2000–2025, where I served as Team Leader & GIS Lead. I later extended that work independently into the Industrial Development Readiness Model (IDRM) and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

I also work across applied geospatial data engineering, including the Wejhatna smart tourism project, where I led the geospatial data layer and prepared multi-source data for a spatial AI decision-support workflow.
